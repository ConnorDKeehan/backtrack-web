### How to play

Firstly register and log in.

Once you've done that you can create a game by clicking the plus button and giving your game a name
<img width="1911" height="937" alt="how-to-create-game" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/555ceef5-ae69-4aae-95c3-cf2e4feac546" />

Once you've joined your newly created game and all of your other friends have done the same. The player's who turn it is can start the round by clicking the "Start Round" text at the bottom of the screen.
<img width="1912" height="939" alt="start-round" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5ac9994e-caaf-4d84-ac95-cf7b54641bdf" />

Then pick your desired playlist
<img width="283" height="415" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/34ae2c45-0e8c-487d-8e02-99b86cacf6f7" />

You will have a timer appear, you have that much time to guess the song
<img width="1916" height="944" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/86cf4bad-6274-4927-9d36-578056d6a04d" />

You can guess by inputting the song and clicking the autocompleted song/artist, and put in a year to guess.

If you get the artist or song correct you get +2, if you get it wrong -2, if you don't guess -1.

For year, you will gain a point if you have the closest guess, and lose one if you have the furthest away guess.
